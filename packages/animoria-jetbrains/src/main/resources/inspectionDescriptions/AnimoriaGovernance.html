Reports Animoria's governance findings on the asset file they concern — unreferenced assets, byte-identical duplicates, oversized files, and disallowed formats.

Every finding carries the evidence behind it, how confident Animoria is, and how much of the workspace the reference scan actually read. Findings are produced by @animoria/core from the rules configured in .animoriarc; this inspection presents them and computes nothing of its own.

No quick fixes are offered: every remediation for these findings removes a file, and removal goes through Animoria's review-and-confirm flow, which shows a preview and stages the removal into a recoverable trash session.
